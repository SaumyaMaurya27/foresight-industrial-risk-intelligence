# Hackathon Presentation Materials

## Purpose
This directory stores resource documents, speaker scripts, and slide structures, helping the team prepare for hackathon evaluations and product demonstrations.

## Outline & Structure
*   **Pitch Tagline:** *Foresight: See Risk Before It Becomes Reality.*
*   **Pitch Deck Contents:**
    1.  **Slide 1: Title & Tagline** (Visual: High-impact refinery aesthetic)
    2.  **Slide 2: The Problem** (Frag-telemetry alerts, industrial accidents, cost of safety failures)
    3.  **Slide 3: Our Solution** (Centralized compound-risk engine, predictive safety scores, AI assistant)
    4.  **Slide 4: System Architecture** (FastAPI, React dashboard, Gemini AI engine, SQLite)
    5.  **Slide 5: Live Demo** (Walkthrough of dashboard, risk alert triggers, and AI Analyst chat panels)
    6.  **Slide 6: Future Vision** (SCADA hooks, digital twins, BLE localization)

## Why It Exists
Separates project promotional plans and demonstration scripts from application source code, allowing team presenters to work on pitch assets without modifying the engineering directories.
